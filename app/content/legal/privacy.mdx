# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 24 October 2026**

Khalai Studio ("Khalai", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains
what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have. It
is written to comply with the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019** (KDPA) and,
for visitors from the European Union, the **General Data Protection Regulation**
(GDPR).

## 1. Who we are (Data Controller)

For the purposes of the KDPA, Khalai Studio is the **data controller**. Our
contact details:

- Email: privacy@khalai.studio
- Location: Nairobi, Kenya

## 2. What personal data we collect

We only collect what we need. Depending on how you use our site, we may collect:

- **Contact form data**: your name, email address, company name (optional),
  phone number (optional), project description, and the time you submitted.
- **Enquiry source data**: when you submit the contact form, we also record
  which page of our site you came from (`pageUrl`), which page referred you
  (`referrer`), which service you were viewing when you clicked through
  (`service` — for example, "seo" or "scraping"), and how you found the form
  (`source` — for example, "marketing-service"). This helps us understand which
  services and pages generate enquiries, and route your message to the right
  place. See section 3 for the lawful basis.
- **Newsletter data**: your email address, subscription status, and confirmation
  timestamps.
- **Account data**: if you create an account, your name, email, phone number
  (optional), age, bio (optional), and a hashed password.
- **Project data**: any information you share with us during a project —
  documents, code, business details.
- **Technical data**: IP address, browser type, and session activity, collected
  for security and basic site functionality. We do not use third-party tracking
  or advertising cookies.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## 3. Why we collect it (Lawful basis)

Under the KDPA, we rely on one or more of these lawful bases:

| Purpose                                                   | Legal basis                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Responding to your contact enquiry                        | Your consent (when you submit the form) |
| Understanding which pages and services generate enquiries | Our legitimate interest                 |
| Delivering a project you hired us for                     | Performance of a contract               |
| Sending you newsletters                                   | Your consent (double opt-in)            |
| Keeping the site secure and working                       | Our legitimate interest                 |
| Complying with tax and legal obligations                  | Legal obligation                        |

**Our legitimate interest in enquiry source data.** When you submit an enquiry,
we record the page you came from and the service you were viewing so we can
understand which parts of our marketing bring real conversations, and improve
the rest. This processing is limited to that purpose, does not affect any of
your rights, and is never shared with third parties for their own marketing. You
can object to it at any time (see section 8).

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent
does not affect the lawfulness of processing done before you withdrew.

## 4. How we use your data

We use personal data to:

- Respond to your enquiries and provide project quotes.
- Understand which services and pages generate enquiries, so we can improve our
  website and marketing.
- Deliver, support, and improve our services.
- Send newsletters you have opted into.
- Keep records required by law (invoices, tax).
- Detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

We do **not** sell your data. We do **not** share it for advertising.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share data only where necessary and only with parties who protect it:

- **Hosting providers**: our site and database are hosted on secure cloud
  infrastructure.
- **Email delivery**: we use transactional email providers (e.g. Resend) to send
  confirmations and notifications.
- **Payment processors**: to take payment for services.
- **Professional advisors**: accountants, lawyers, where required.
- **Authorities**: when required by law or to protect our rights.

Each processor is contractually bound to use your data only for the agreed
purpose.

## 6. Cross-border data transfers

Khalai Studio is based in Kenya. Some of our service providers store or process
data outside Kenya. Under the KDPA, we ensure that any transfer outside Kenya
has adequate safeguards: either a legal instrument with the recipient, an
adequacy decision from the Data Commissioner, or your explicit consent.

If you are in the EU/EEA, we ensure transfers comply with GDPR requirements
(Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent).

## 7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it:

- **Contact enquiries**: up to 24 months after the last contact, unless you
  become a client.
- **Enquiry source data** (service, source, referrer, page URL): retained for 24
  months alongside the enquiry for reporting and analysis, then deleted or
  aggregated so it no longer identifies you.
- **Client project data**: for the duration of the project plus 7 years (for tax
  and legal records).
- **Newsletter subscriptions**: until you unsubscribe, plus a short period to
  honour the request.
- **Account data**: until you delete your account, plus a short retention window
  for backups.

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

## 8. Your rights

Under the KDPA and, where applicable, GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** data we no longer need.
- **Restrict** or **object** to processing.
- **Port** your data to another service (where technically possible).
- **Withdraw consent** at any time.
- **Complain** to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) in
  Kenya, or your local supervisory authority if you are in the EU/EEA.

**Right to object to lead tracking.** If you would prefer that we do not record
the page or service you came from when you submit an enquiry, email us at
privacy@khalai.studio and we will delete that information from your enquiry
record. Your message will still be handled — the only thing you are opting out
of is the record of where it came from.

To exercise any of these rights, email **privacy@khalai.studio**. We will
respond within 30 days. We may ask you to verify your identity first.

## 9. Security

We protect personal data with:

- Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
- Hashed passwords (bcrypt).
- Access controls and least-privilege permissions.
- Regular software updates and vulnerability checks.
- Contractual safeguards with processors.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your rights, we will notify
the ODPC and affected individuals as required by law.

## 10. Cookies and local storage

We use only essential cookies and local storage for:

- Keeping you signed in.
- Remembering your theme preference.
- Preventing form abuse (rate limiting).

When you submit an enquiry, the page you came from and the service you were
viewing are captured from the URL and sent with the form. This is not a cookie
or local storage — it is data that travels with your submission and is stored
only with your enquiry record.

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If this changes, we
will update this policy and ask for your consent.

## 11. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect
their data. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be marked with a new "last
updated" date. Continuing to use the site means you accept the revised policy.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to:

- **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)**, Kenya -
